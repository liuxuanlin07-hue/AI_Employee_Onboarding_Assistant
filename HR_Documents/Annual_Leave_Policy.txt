Annual Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per year.

2. Leave Application

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 3 working days before the planned leave date.

3. Carry Forward

Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days to the next calendar year.

4. Leave Approval

Annual leave is subject to manager approval and business needs.

5. New Employees

Employees who join during the year will receive annual leave on a pro-rated basis.

6. Unused Leave

Unused annual leave above the 5-day carry-forward limit will expire at the end of the year.
