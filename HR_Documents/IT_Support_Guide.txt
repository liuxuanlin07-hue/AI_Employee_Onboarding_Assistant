IT Support Guide

1. Password Reset

Employees who forget their company password should use the self-service password reset portal.

2. IT Help Desk

If the password reset portal does not work, employees should contact the IT Help Desk.

The IT Help Desk is available from Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

3. Email Access

New employees will receive their company email account on their first working day.

4. Laptop Support

Employees who have problems with their company laptop should contact the IT Help Desk and provide their employee ID and device number.

5. Software Installation

Employees should not install unapproved software on company devices.

If special software is required for work, employees should submit a request to the IT Help Desk.

6. Cybersecurity

Employees should not share passwords with other people and should report suspicious emails to the IT Security Team.
