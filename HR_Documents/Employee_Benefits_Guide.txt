Employee Benefits Guide

1. Medical Insurance

All full-time employees are covered by the company medical insurance plan from their first day of employment.

2. Dental Benefits

Employees can claim up to SGD 500 per year for eligible dental expenses.

3. Health Screening

The company provides one basic health screening for employees each year.

4. Wellness Allowance

Employees can claim up to SGD 300 per year for approved wellness activities, including gym memberships and fitness classes.

5. Family Benefits

Employees may receive additional medical coverage for eligible dependants, subject to the company's insurance plan.

6. Benefit Enquiries

Employees who have questions about benefits should contact the HR Benefits Team.
